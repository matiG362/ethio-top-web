About us

Ethio Top Paints

Ethio Top Manufacturing PLC is a leading Ethiopian manufacturer of high-quality paint products established in Addis Ababa, in 2013 Ethiopian calendar with brand name Ethio top paints, specializing in both interior and exterior wall paints. We are driven by a mission to provide durable, Eco-friendly, and aesthetically pleasing paint solutions to homes, businesses, and industrial projects throughout Ethiopia and beyond.

Our Mission

To produce top-quality, affordable, and sustainable paint solutions that enhance the beauty and longevity of buildings while exceeding customer expectations by leveraging technology, skilled and disciplined personnel .

Our Vision

To be one of the top three Ethiopia’s most trusted and innovative paint brand quality manufacturing companies in the country in the next five years.
