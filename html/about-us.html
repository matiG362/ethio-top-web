About us

Ethio Top Paints

At Ethio Top Paints, we believe that color is more than just paint on a wall-it's an expression of creativity, identity, and lifestyle. Established with a vision to revolutionize the Ethiopian paint industry, we specialize in producing high-quality, durable, and eco-friendly paints tailored to the unique needs of Ethiopian homes, businesses, and industries.
With years of expertise and a passion for innovation, we provide a wide range of interior, exterior, wood, metal, and decorative paints that ensure lasting beauty and protection for every surface.

Our Mission

To enhance living spaces with high-quality, durable, and eco-friendly paints, ensuring beauty and protection for every surface.

Our Vision

To be Ethiopia's leading paint brand, known for innovation, sustainability, and unmatched quality.
