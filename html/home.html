FAQ

Quick Answers to Common questions

How long does your paint take to dry, and when can I apply a second coat?
Drying times can vary depending on the type of paint, the ambient temperature, humidity levels, and the thickness of the application. It's crucial to allow the first coat to dry thoroughly before applying the second to ensure proper adhesion and the best possible finish. Always refer to the product label for specific drying times, as they can vary between different paint types within our range.
Are your paints washable and durable, especially for interior walls?
Yes, many of our interior emulsion paints are formulated for excellent washability and durability. Our product descriptions will clearly indicate the level of washability, ranging from wipeable to highly scrubbable. We use high-quality pigments and binders in our formulations to ensure that the color remains vibrant and the paint film can withstand regular cleaning without damage.
WHow do your paints perform in the Ethiopian climate, particularly against sun and rain?
Our exterior paints are specifically formulated to excel in the Ethiopian climate. We understand the challenges of intense sunlight and seasonal rains, and our formulations are designed to directly addresses the climatic conditions in Ethiopia, showing an understanding of local needs.
